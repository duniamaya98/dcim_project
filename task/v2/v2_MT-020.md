## 3. Architecture

```
Raw Metrics
     ↓
Drift Detection (Z-score)
     ↓
DomainEngine (domain scoring)
     ↓
CorrelationBuffer (rolling window)
     ↓
AggregationEngine (temporal aggregation)
     ↓
CorrelationEngine (cross-domain correlation)
     ↓
IncidentBuilder (incident construction)
     ↓
SeverityMatrix (severity classification)
```
